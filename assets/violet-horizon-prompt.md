# Purple twilight wallpaper

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Original generated output is preserved; the image in this folder is the wallpaper asset. Android applies a dark color filter at display time. Animated gradients remain paused while a custom wallpaper is active.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 16:9 landscape wallpaper for a television home dashboard, ideally 3840 x 2160 pixels. Create an elegant minimalist purple mountain-and-water landscape at twilight, designed to sit behind white dashboard typography. Deep aubergine and midnight indigo dominate; soft muted violet and lavender atmospheric light with restrained mauve highlights. Silky layered distant mountain silhouettes, subtle mist and a very soft reflection on calm water. A broad gentle sweep of violet light across the lower middle gives depth without becoming bright. The image must be beautiful as a standalone wallpaper but subdued, with plentiful quiet negative space: the left third behind sports/weather text must be very dark and uncluttered; the central upper and middle area behind a large analog/digital clock must be smooth deep purple with low contrast; the right fifth behind a flight radar must also remain dark and quiet. Keep the main landscape contours low in the image. High quality clean polished illustration, smooth color transitions, tasteful cinematic atmosphere, no distracting fine details, no texture noise or banding. This is ONLY the wallpaper, absolutely no text, numbers, clocks, widgets, cards, interfaces, borders, logos, watermarks, bright sun, giant moon, or stars.
